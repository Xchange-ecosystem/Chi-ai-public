## 3. Rewarded Open Source (rOS) Terms

“Rewarded Open Source” (rOS) refers to a model where contributions and uses within the **Chi-AI ecosystem**  
may be tracked, attributed, and rewarded (e.g., via credits, tokens, or recognition systems).  

Use of licensed materials within this ecosystem implies consent to:

- Participate in the shared attribution system  
- Allow derivative works to be discoverable and reusable under the same rOS terms  
- Accept that future versions of the Chi-AI license may evolve to improve the reward and attribution mechanisms

Co-working inside the ecosystem follows organised and sychronised DevOps, in which calls for modules or services required to advance the ecosystem is extended to the community ("Request for Contribution", RFC), members sign up for the task and are awared rewards for successfully contributing. Modules and services can be suggested by the community members and are subject to DAO-voting.
